    About CatchUp

    Built for the PROTOCOL X hackathon by team PALS_.

    What it does

    CatchUp uses a genuine pretrained AI model (Llama 3.2 1B) running entirely in your browser via WebLLM/WebGPU. Paste any conversation and get an instant breakdown of what needs your attention, action items, decisions, and open questions.

    Privacy first

    Local Private and Instant Rules process conversation text on your device. Local Private downloads model weights when first used. Cloud Turbo sends the transcript directly to your selected API provider using your saved key.

    How it works

    1. Paste or load a sample conversation
    2. Choose Local Private, Cloud Turbo, or Instant Rules
    3. The selected engine analyzes the conversation; Local Private downloads its model on first use
    4. AI findings require exact quotes from the cited messages; classifications can still be imperfect
