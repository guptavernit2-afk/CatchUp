    About CatchUp

    Your personal workspace for catching up on conversations and keeping track of what matters.

    What it does

    Turn a conversation into a briefing, tasks, decisions, and open questions. Add your name to highlight items that need you. Paste a transcript, upload a .txt or .md file, or try the sample to get started.

    Choose how to process

    Local Private keeps your conversation on this device and downloads a model on first use; it needs a compatible WebGPU browser. Instant Rules works on-device without a model download. Cloud Turbo sends your transcript to your chosen provider using your own API key, saved in this browser.

    Read your results

    1. Start with the summary, then explore tasks, decisions, and open questions.
    2. Requested tasks await acceptance. Confirmed tasks have a commitment or acceptance in the conversation.
    3. Checkboxes mark completion for this report. Use Save changes to update a saved catch-up, or copy/download Markdown to keep a separate copy.
    4. Use the quoted text and message references to verify findings. Extraction can miss context or classify a message incorrectly.
